Keep this installer private. It includes data from your PC copy.

Then, on your 3DS

Copy your CIA to cias on the SD card. Open FBI → SD → cias and install it. Keep at least 4 GB free before copying.

Updating? Install over the existing title. Back up 3ds/simpsons first; keep your saves and 3ds/dspfirm.cdc.

Before you start

Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Allow at least 8 GB of free disk space for temporary files. Keep this tab open while it builds.

Building can take time, especially when converting the movies.

An unofficial homebrew project. Requires your own PC game files and a 3DS with custom firmware.
