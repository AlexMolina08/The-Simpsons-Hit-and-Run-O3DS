# The Simpsons: Hit & Run for Nintendo 3DS v1.2.0

This update focuses on fast driving, a more consistent world and improved presentation on Nintendo 3DS and 2DS, including the original models.

- **Smoother world loading across all seven levels.** Exterior roads and scenery stay in memory, greatly reducing missing map sections and late scenery pop-in when travelling quickly. Distant landmarks also remain visible farther away.
- **Improved performance and frame pacing.** Reduce rendering, animation and resource-lookup overhead, and give pending world loads time during frame pacing. Normal builds keep development diagnostics and profiling disabled.
- **Sharper graphics.** 800×240 Quality Mode on supported models, prepared world lighting, character shading, vehicle reflections and refreshed handheld HUD artwork.
- **Enhanced stereoscopic 3D.** Refined depth, steadier camera transitions and better foreground separation, with corrected fog rendering. The console slider switches between stereo and flat output during play.
- **More reliable gameplay.** Fix crashes during mission restarts and level changes, retain mission-character models during long approaches and stop parked-car suspension oscillation. Vehicle-entry transitions use black instead of white.
- **Correct intro-video rendering.** Black borders and the lower screen remain black in both 2D and stereo.
- **Scrollable mission selection.** All seven missions fit through controller scrolling or stylus dragging, with matching touch selection.
- **Improved loading audio.** Prioritize streamed music and dialogue reads during world-data validation.
- **Browser CIA builder.** Prepare a complete installer locally from an installed PC copy or supported disc images, with localized text, dialogue and cinematic audio. Includes stronger input checks and verified resumable conversion stages.
- **Startup details.** Show v1.2.0 and the port credit while retaining the original legal artwork.

800×240 is horizontal wide output in 2D; stereo uses 400×240 per eye. The original 2DS remains at 400×240. Frame rates vary by scene, and some distance culling, LOD changes and dynamic-object pop-in remain.

## Install or update

Create your installer with the [browser builder](https://alexmolina08.github.io/The-Simpsons-Hit-and-Run-O3DS/) using your own PC copy, then install with FBI. Before updating, back up `sd:/3ds/simpsons/`, then install over the existing title without uninstalling it. Keep your saves, settings and `sd:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc`. No separate game-data copy is needed. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/AlexMolina08/The-Simpsons-Hit-and-Run-O3DS/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

You need your own copy of the original PC game. English, French and Spanish are supported.
